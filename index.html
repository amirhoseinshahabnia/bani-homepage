Why Marketing by Bani

I learned marketing inside the legal world, not around it.

I started in 2017 with a camera and a stack of novels. Bani’s Book Club taught me something I have relied on ever since: people come back to the person they trust to tell them what is worth their time.

Then I spent more than five years inside the legal industry. As Client Services Director at an estate planning firm, I spoke with more than 1,000 families and worked alongside attorneys, close enough to see how carefully you choose your words, and how quickly the wrong marketing can undo a reputation that took twenty years to build.

That’s why I only work with estate planning attorneys, and why I don’t sell volume. This is a conversation about mortality, family, and money. I practice ethical marketing, which means one thing in this field: we never use a family’s fear to pressure them into planning.

What I do instead is slower and, in my experience, considerably more durable: one message, a team that runs everything around it, and me on every call.

Those conversations are where the idea behind this practice came from. The families who booked had usually been following an attorney for weeks before they called.

  • Legal-industry background. I know what you can and can’t say.
  • Estate planning only. One specialty, every client.
  • A deliberately small client list. You always talk to me.

Are we the right fit?

We work with a select group of estate planning attorneys, because a reputation that took years to build deserves marketing that’s done right.

We are a fit if you:

  • Practice estate planning, and only estate planning
  • Are ready to be the face of your practice

And at least one of these:

  • Rely on referrals and want a second source of clients
  • Have tried an agency and been burned
  • Want to run webinars but not build them
  • Have a website that doesn’t bring in consistent inquiries
  • Are tired of consultations that go nowhere

We are not a fit if you:

  • Are looking for the lowest price
  • Practice six areas of law and estate planning is one of them
  • Aren’t ready to commit to at least three months
  • Want results next week
